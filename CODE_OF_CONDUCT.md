# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in FlashPlay welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus criticism on code, design, evidence, and outcomes.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes without hostility.
- Protect private information and security reports.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and other unprofessional conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or GitHub reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the project community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
